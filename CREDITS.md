# Credits

The YouTube skills here were rewritten from scratch, in Mr.V's own words, from methods in
Serge Bulaev's MIT-licensed [youtube-skills](https://github.com/sergebulaev/youtube-skills). The method and
quality bar are his; the wording, structure and examples are new. His copyright notice is kept in
[LICENSE](LICENSE) as the MIT License requires.

Differences from the original:
- Publishing runs through [Postiz](https://postiz.com) instead of Publora.
- No paid read layer (Apify) or image API: the skills work from what you paste or what Claude can read.

Postiz (AGPL-3.0) is called over its public API. No Postiz code is included here.
